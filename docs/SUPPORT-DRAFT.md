## Get riding

Choose a rider and world, then start **Weekly Challenge** or **Endless**. All nine riders share the same physics.

- Right pedal: accelerate on the ground and lean back in the air.
- Left pedal: brake on the ground and lean forward in the air.
- Hold a pedal and slide your thumb down to reduce its strength. Release to let momentum carry you.

Land with the slope. Flips count only after a safe landing.

The weekly course is 4,000 m with one life and no time limit. It changes every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Endless gives you three lives. Both Endless and weekly practice work offline.

## Practice and online scores

Game Center is optional. Use **Settings > Connect Game Center** in CrocoCross, or the device's **Settings > Game Center**, to sign in. A ranked weekly run needs an active, confirmed weekly competition. If it is unavailable or has not opened yet, you can still practice. [Apple's Game Center settings guide](https://support.apple.com/en-nz/guide/iphone/iph3b2f23e83/ios).

Start a new ranked run after connecting. Runs started as practice are not uploaded later. Finish the full weekly course before the competition ends to submit a weekly score and time. Failed submissions can retry with the original account while that week remains open; expired results are not moved to the next week. Local records and online leaderboard entries are separate.

Game Center controls your public profile and leaderboard visibility. For global leaderboards, your nickname, avatar and scores can be visible to other players. Review sharing settings and [Apple's Game Center privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/).

## Pausing, saves and sound

Tap Pause to stop your ride. Leaving the app or changing the play window can also pause it. Choose **Keep riding** to resume. An unfinished saved ride is available through **Continue your ride** on the home screen; starting a replacement ride overwrites it.

For sound, check your device's silent setting and the Music, Engine and Effects sliders in CrocoCross Settings. Music and haptic feedback can be turned off separately. After a headphone or audio interruption, use the music Play button or resume the ride to restore playback when appropriate.

## Removing local data

To remove this installation's settings, records, unfinished ride and unsent scores, use **Delete App** in the device's storage settings. Offloading preserves documents and data. [Apple's storage guide](https://support.apple.com/en-au/108429).

Deleting CrocoCross does not erase submitted Game Center scores or existing device backups. Manage Game Center and backups through Apple; CrocoCross has no separate cloud-save account. See [Game Center & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/) and [iCloud Backup information](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/108770).

## Report a problem

Contact us using the details above. Include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version or TestFlight build, game mode, and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot or short recording can help. Never send your Apple Account password or verification codes.

CrocoCross has no developer-operated game backend, ads or analytics SDK. We cannot inspect your device's saved ride remotely; the details you choose to provide help us investigate.
