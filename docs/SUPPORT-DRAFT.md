## Get riding

Use the **Rider** and **World** selectors, then start **Weekly Challenge** or **Endless**. All nine riders share the same physics. A new ride starts immediately.

- Right pedal: accelerate on the ground and lean back in the air.
- Left pedal: brake on the ground and lean forward in the air.
- Hold a pedal and slide your thumb down to reduce its strength. Release to let momentum carry you.

Land with the slope. Flips count only after a safe landing.

The weekly course is 2,600 m with one life and no time limit. It changes every Monday at 00:00 UTC. If you crash before the finish, your points are displayed as an unvalidated score and do not count toward your Weekly record or rankings. Endless gives you three lives. Both Endless and weekly practice work offline.

## Practice and online scores

Game Center is optional. Use **Rankings > Connect** in CrocoCross, or the device's **Settings > Game Center**, to sign in. A ranked weekly run needs an active, confirmed weekly competition. If it is unavailable or has not opened yet, you can still practice. Connection and leaderboard status appear in Rankings; there is no practice-status label during play. [Apple's Game Center settings guide](https://support.apple.com/en-nz/guide/iphone/iph3b2f23e83/ios).

Start a new ranked run after connecting. Runs started as practice are not uploaded later. Finish the full weekly course before the competition ends to submit a weekly score and time. Failed submissions can retry with the original account while that week remains open; expired results are not moved to the next week. Local records and online leaderboard entries are separate.

Game Center controls your public profile and leaderboard visibility. For global leaderboards, your nickname, avatar and scores can be visible to other players. Review sharing settings and [Apple's Game Center privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/).

## Pausing, leaving a ride and sound

Tap **Pause** to stop your ride, then choose **Keep riding** at the bottom of the pause card to resume. A temporary system interruption or a change to the play window can also pause the ride in memory. **Restart** begins a new ride immediately, without confirmation.

Returning **Home** or sending CrocoCross to the background abandons the current ride. Reopening the app starts at Home; there is no Continue option. Your settings, personal records and eligible pending online scores remain saved.

Open **Settings > Audio** to turn **Play music** on or off, choose **Playlist** or **One track**, select a track, use **Mute all sound**, and adjust the **Music volume**, **Engine** and **Effects** sliders. Haptic feedback is in **Settings > General**. Audio has no separate play/pause, next or previous buttons.

If sound is missing, check Play music, mute, the volume levels, your device's silent setting and its selected audio output. After a headphone or system interruption, return to the app and turn Play music off and on if you want to resume music.

## Removing local data

To remove this installation's settings, records and unsent scores, use **Delete App** in the device's storage settings. Offloading preserves documents and data. [Apple's storage guide](https://support.apple.com/en-au/108429).

Deleting CrocoCross does not erase submitted Game Center scores or existing device backups. Manage Game Center and backups through Apple; CrocoCross has no separate cloud-save account. See [Game Center & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/) and [iCloud Backup information](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/108770).

## Report a problem

Contact us using the details above. Include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version or TestFlight build, game mode, and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot or short recording can help. Never send your Apple Account password or verification codes.

CrocoCross has no developer-operated game backend, ads or analytics SDK. We cannot inspect your device or gameplay remotely; the details you choose to provide help us investigate.
