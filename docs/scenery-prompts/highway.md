# Highway / American Sunset scenery

Six PNG illustrations created with the built-in image_gen tool, one call per image, after inspecting `App/Resources/GameAssets/american-sunset.png`. Final PNGs copied unchanged from generated files; no scripted cutout, recoloring or image alteration.

## Delivered concepts

- ground-1.png: cream/turquoise vintage caravan on a small dry ground patch.
- ground-2.png: sleeping desert coyote.
- ground-3.png: teal vintage pickup with pumpkins.
- sky-1.png: cream/orange vintage propeller airplane, heading right.
- sky-2.png: red-tailed hawk in flight, heading right.
- wayside.png: low old tires with yellow desert wildflowers; no post or sign.

Final folder: `App/Resources/GameAssets/Scenery/highway/`.

## Visual and alpha QA

All six final images were visually opened using view_image. Subjects are complete, recognizable, warm sunset colored and individually isolated. No text, border, sign or regular roadside pole. All six contain real RGBA alpha with hundreds of thousands of alpha-zero pixels. No painted checkerboard in any delivered PNG. Ground images and hawk: 1536×1024, alpha range 0–254. Airplane and wayside: 1774×887, alpha range 0–255.

Known limitation: `ground-1.png`, `ground-2.png`, `ground-3.png` and `sky-2.png` retain a broad soft warm semi-transparent halo around the subjects. Four targeted background-extraction tool edits returned RGB checkerboards and were rejected. One further brand-new generation per affected subject retained essentially the same warm aura; initial RGBA images were selected for their superior subject drawing. Parent agent informed and will assess at actual in-game size. Airplane and wayside have clean transparent cutouts. No game-level visual QA was performed by this asset-only agent.
