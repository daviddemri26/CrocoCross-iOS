Quick guide

How do the controls work?

Hold the right grip to accelerate and lean back. Hold the left lever to brake and lean forward. Release to coast. Use short presses to adjust your angle and land with the slope.

How do I score?

Each metre earns 10 points. Land a single flip for 1,000 points, a double for 3,000, or a triple for 7,000. Backflips and frontflips have the same rewards. Figures count only after a safe landing. Finishing Weekly adds 1,000 points.

Weekly or Endless?

Weekly is a 4,000-metre course, one life, no time limit. It changes Monday at 00:00 UTC. Endless gives you three lives and has no finish line.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Local riding and records work offline. Apple Game Center leaderboards require an internet connection, an eligible authenticated run and active leaderboard configuration. You can keep playing locally if Game Center is unavailable.

Why did my run end when I left the app?

Rides are not saved. Pause keeps a ride in memory while the app remains open. Returning Home or leaving the app ends the run. Your local best scores and settings remain.

How do I change the sound?

Use the speaker button on Home to mute or unmute all sounds. Settings opens Audio, with music, engine and effects volumes, track selection and music options. Haptics are in General.
