CrocoCross is a native side-view motorcycle game for iPhone and iPad. The interface is in English. No separate app account, demo credentials, purchase or subscription is required.

Start with Rocco and Canyon. Choose Weekly for a shared 2,600 m course with one life, or Endless for a fresh open-ended trail with three lives. Hold the right control area to accelerate and lean back; hold the left to brake and lean forward. Controls settle beneath the initial touch. Short presses provide finer control. How to explains the controls and scoring.

Flips count only after a safe landing. Kenji unlocks after 50 safely landed backflips, and Japan Mountains after 50 safely landed frontflips. Progress persists across rides, including after a fall. Tap the ready catalog card to claim an unlock. Other planned catalog entries remain unavailable.

Achievements shows 40 local achievements. Double and triple jumps require two or three rotations safely landed in one jump. Forward and backward rotations contribute to shared cumulative totals. Rankings shows separate Weekly score and time records; its Endless link opens per-world records in the world catalog. Completed Weekly course records require reaching the finish.

Game Center is optional. Both modes remain playable locally without authentication. An eligible Weekly run requires a confirmed active competition at the start and a full finish before points/time can be submitted. Endless rankings are separate for Canyon and Japan. Game Center uses Apple's sign-in and app-scoped player identity; no additional login is required.

Home has a sound toggle. Settings > Audio provides separate music, engine and effects volumes. The app has no background-audio entitlement. Returning Home abandons the current run while preserving local records, achievements, unlock progress and preferences.

Cartoon falls and the final-life explosion contain no blood or gore. Reduce Motion limits decorative effects. Third-party licenses and credits are available in Settings > About.
