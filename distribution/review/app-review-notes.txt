CrocoCross is a native side-view riding game for iPhone and iPad. The interface is in English. No separate app account, demo credentials, purchase or subscription is required.

This update adds Milo, a monkey riding an electric scooter, and Tropical Jungle. Start with Rocco and Canyon. Choose Weekly for a shared 2,600 m Canyon course with one life, or Endless for a fresh open-ended trail with three lives. Hold the right control area to accelerate and lean back; hold the left to brake and lean forward. Controls settle beneath the initial touch. Short presses provide finer control. How to explains the controls and scoring.

Flips count only after a safe landing. Kenji unlocks after 50 safely landed backflips, Milo after 100 backflips, Japan Mountains after 50 frontflips and Tropical Jungle after 100 frontflips. Progress accumulates across modes and rides, including rides subsequently lost or abandoned. Existing progress and claims are preserved. Tap each ready rider/world card to claim its unlock. Other planned catalog entries, including Duke, Paris and Arctic Aurora, remain unavailable.

Tropical Jungle has grassy platforms, physical gaps and cliff walls, ramp launches and horizontal departures. The camera follows the rider closely. Recovery uses the normal controls and momentum; there is no automatic jump boost. Its Endless personal records are separate from Canyon and Japan.

Achievements shows 40 local achievements. Game Center achievement synchronization is disabled; the local achievement panel remains fully available. Double and triple jumps require two or three rotations safely landed in one jump. Forward and backward rotations contribute to shared cumulative totals. No new dedicated Milo or Jungle achievement is added.

Game Center is optional. Both modes remain playable locally without authentication. Weekly points/time can be submitted only after an eligible run begins in a confirmed active competition and reaches the finish. Weekly changes every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Endless rankings are separate for Canyon, Japan and Tropical Jungle. An unavailable world leaderboard leaves local play available. Game Center uses Apple's sign-in and app-scoped player identity; no additional login is required.

Home and Pause have the same sound toggle; muting does not resume a paused ride. Settings > Audio provides separate music, engine and effects volumes. The app has no background-audio entitlement. Returning Home abandons the current run while preserving local records, achievements, unlock progress and preferences.

Cartoon falls and the final-life explosion contain no blood or gore. Reduce Motion limits decorative effects. Third-party licenses and credits are available in Settings > About.
