Meet Milo and explore Tropical Jungle.

- Unlock Milo, a monkey riding an electric scooter, by safely landing 100 backflips.
- Unlock Tropical Jungle by safely landing 100 frontflips.
- Ride grassy platforms, clear larger gaps and mix ramp launches with flat takeoffs.
- Discover a closer Jungle camera, scrolling forest scenery and large waterfalls.
- Mute or unmute the game directly from Pause.
- Keep separate Endless records for Canyon, Japan and Tropical Jungle.

Your existing unlock progress and personal records are preserved.
