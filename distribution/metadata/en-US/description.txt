One more hill. One cleaner landing. One more ride.

Meet Rocco, a crocodile with a motorcycle and a Canyon to explore. CrocoCross is a side-view riding game about momentum, balance and landing the next jump.

FIND YOUR FLOW
Accelerate down rolling trails, catch air over bumps, and use your balance to line up the landing. The motorcycle and rider move separately, with suspension that reacts to the ground.

TWO THUMBS. PLENTY TO MASTER.
Hold the right grip to accelerate and lean back. Hold the left lever to brake and lean forward. Use short presses for finer control, on the ground and in the air.

A NEW WEEKLY TRAIL
Take on a 4,000-metre course with one life and no time limit. The trail changes every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Learn the hills and try to reach the finish.

KEEP GOING IN ENDLESS
Three lives, an open-ended trail, and another chance to beat your local best. Travel farther and land backflips or frontflips to build your score. Flips count only after a safe landing.

YOUR RIDE, YOUR SOUND
Choose from three original music tracks, adjust music, engine and effects separately, or mute everything with one tap. Optional haptics add feedback under your thumbs.

Play Endless and the weekly trail offline. Local records and preferences stay on your device. The interface is in English.

Free to play. No ads. No in-app purchases. No separate CrocoCross account required.
