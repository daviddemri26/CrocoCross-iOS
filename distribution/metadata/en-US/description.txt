One more hill. One cleaner landing. One more ride.

CrocoCross is a side-view motorcycle game about momentum, balance and landing the next jump. Start with Rocco the crocodile in the Canyon, then earn new ways to ride.

MEET KENJI. EXPLORE JAPAN.
Safely land 50 backflips to unlock Kenji, a Shiba Inu on a blue-and-white motorcycle. Land 50 frontflips to unlock Japan Mountains, with sharper ridges, a wide Mount Fuji panorama and towering torii gates. Progress carries across rides, even after a fall.

TWO THUMBS. PLENTY TO MASTER.
Hold the right side to accelerate and lean back. Hold the left to brake and lean forward. The controls settle beneath your thumbs, with short presses for finer control. Suspension and an articulated rider bring each jump, landing and tumble to life.

A SHARED WEEKLY CHALLENGE
Take on a 2,600-metre course with one life and no time limit. Everyone rides the same weekly route, changing every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Reach the finish to record your score and time.

FRESH ENDLESS RIDES
Three lives and a new trail each ride. Travel farther, safely land flips and chase a separate personal best in each available world.

40 ACHIEVEMENTS TO CHASE
Track local progress through first flips, double and triple jumps, cumulative rotations, distance milestones up to 50 km, Weekly finishes and new unlocks. Flips count only after a safe landing.

YOUR RIDE, YOUR SOUND
Choose from three original music tracks, adjust music, engine and effects separately, or mute everything with one tap. Optional haptics add feedback under your thumbs.

Play Weekly and Endless offline. Local records, unlock progress, achievements and preferences stay on your device. Game Center is optional. The interface is in English.

Free to play. No ads. No in-app purchases. No separate CrocoCross account required.
